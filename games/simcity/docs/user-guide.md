# SimCity 3D — User Guide

## Objective
Grow a thriving 3D metropolis from a small Hamlet to a Megalopolis (`1,800+` population) while keeping citizens happy, utilities balanced, and the city treasury solvent.

## Getting Started
1. Place a **Power Plant (⚡, `6`)** and **Water Tower (💧, `7`)** beside the entry road.
2. Extend **Roads (`2`)**—or **Bridges (`$30`)** across water—to carry utilities and traffic.
3. Zone **Residential (`3`)**, **Commercial (`4`)**, and **Industrial (`5`)** lots adjacent to roads according to the **R / C / I** demand bars.
4. Add **Parks (`8`)** to offset industrial pollution and boost land value, and place a **Civic Plaza (`9`)** to unlock Level 3–4 skyscrapers.

## Controls
- **Left Click / Drag:** Inspect tile or place selected tool
- **Right Drag / `Q` & `E`:** Orbit 3D camera
- **`WASD` / Arrow Keys:** Pan across the map
- **Mouse Wheel / `+` & `−`:** Zoom in and out
- **Hotkeys `1–9`, `B`:** Switch tools · **`Space`:** Pause / Resume simulation

## Modes, Hints & Persistence
- Click **Tutorial** for a guided 5-step walkthrough, **💡 Hint** for Mayor Advisor recommendations, or **? Help** for an in-game reference.
- Click **New City** to choose `Riverside Valley`, `Twin Islands`, `Green Plains`, or **Load Starter Town**.
- Your city, treasury, lighting preference, and Mute toggle persist automatically in `localStorage` (`simcity_v1`).
