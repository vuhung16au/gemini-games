# Battle City (1985) — Famicom / NES

A faithful browser recreation of Namco's 1985 arcade/Famicom classic **Battle City**, featuring **1-Player** and **2-Player Local Co-op** modes.

## Key Features
- **Classic 13×13 (26×26 Sub-Tile) Arena:** Destructible half-tile Brick walls, Steel armor, Forest cover, Water barriers, slippery Ice, and Eagle HQ defense.
- **1-Player & 2-Player Local Co-op:** Play solo (`WASD` / `Arrows` + `Space` / `Enter`) or team up locally (`P1: WASD + Space`, `P2: Arrows + Enter`) with authentic 2.5s friendly-fire stun.
- **4 Enemy Classes & 6 Power-Ups:** Basic, Fast, Power, and 4-HP Armored tanks; Star (`★` tiers 1–3), Helmet, Clock, Shovel, Grenade, and 1UP Tank.
- **10 Handcrafted Stages + Tutorial & Hints:** Stage Select (`1–10`), between-stage kill tallies, 4-step interactive Tutorial, and live tactical Hints.
