# Battle City (1985) — User Guide

## Objective
Defend your **Eagle HQ (🦅)** at the bottom-center of the arena while destroying all **20 enemy tanks** in each stage. If any shell—enemy or friendly—hits the Eagle, the game ends immediately.

## Controls
- **1-Player Mode:** `WASD` or `Arrow Keys` to drive · `Space`, `Enter`, or `F` to fire (on-screen D-Pad + Fire on touch devices).
- **2-Player Local Co-op:**
  - **Player 1 (Gold Tank):** `WASD` to drive · `Space` or `F` to fire.
  - **Player 2 (Green Tank):** `Arrow Keys` to drive · `Enter` or `/` to fire.
  - *Friendly Fire:* Shooting your partner stuns them for 2.5 seconds without costing a life.
- **Pause:** `P` or `Escape`.

## Power-Ups (+500 pts)
- **★ Star:** Upgrades your tank (1★ fast shot, 2★ double-shot, 3★ breaks Steel walls)
- **⛑ Helmet:** Temporary invulnerability shield
- **⏱ Clock:** Freezes all enemies for 10 seconds
- **⛏ Shovel:** Upgrades Eagle HQ walls to Steel for 15 seconds
- **💣 Grenade:** Destroys all active enemies on screen
- **1UP Tank:** Grants +1 extra life

## Persistence
High score, highest reached stage, and Mute state are saved automatically in `localStorage` (`battle_city_v1`).
