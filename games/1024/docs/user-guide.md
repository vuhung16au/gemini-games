# User Guide — 1024

## Objective
Slide tiles on the **4×4** board to merge matching numbers (`1 + 1 = 2`, `2 + 2 = 4`, …) until you create the **1024** tile.

## Controls
- **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** — Slide all tiles in that direction.
- **Touch / Mouse Swipe** — Swipe anywhere on the board.
- **Z** — **Undo** up to 10 previous moves.
- **H** — Show a **Smart Hint** direction.

## Assist Tools & Settings
- **? Help:** Opens the in-game rules and shortcut modal.
- **Tutorial:** Launches a 3-step guided practice board (`1+1→2`, `2+2→4`, `4+4→8`).
- **Keep Going:** Once you reach `1024`, choose **Keep Going** to keep chasing higher scores.
- **Persistence:** Your best score and mute preference are saved automatically in `localStorage`.
