# User Guide — Tá Lả (Phỏm)

## Objective

Form valid **Phỏm** melds and minimize your remaining **deadwood points (Điểm rác)** across 4 rounds—or win immediately by achieving **Ù** (0 deadwood).

## Valid Phỏm Melds

- **Phỏm Sáp (Set):** 3 or 4 cards of the same rank (e.g., `7♠ 7♥ 7♦`).
- **Phỏm Dây (Run):** 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g., `A♠ 2♠ 3♠`, `9♥ 10♥ J♥`). Note: `Q-K-A` does not wrap.

## Card Point Values

- `A` = 1 pt
- `2–10` = face value (2–10 pts)
- `J` = 11 pts, `Q` = 12 pts, `K` = 13 pts

## Turn Flow

1. **Ăn / Eat** or **Bốc / Draw:** Start your turn by taking the previous player's just-discarded card if it forms a valid Phỏm with cards in your hand, or draw 1 card from the **Nọc** pile. (The starting dealer begins with 10 cards and skips drawing on Turn 1.)
2. **Đánh / Discard:** Select 1 legal card (eaten cards cannot be discarded) and click **Đánh / Play** or double-click the card.
3. **Round 4 — Hạ Phỏm & Gửi bài / Meld & Lay Off:** Lay down your optimal Phỏms, optionally lay off remaining deadwood cards onto earlier melds (unless you are **Móm** with 0 Phỏms), and discard your final card.

## Game Modes & Controls

- **Player Count:** `2P`, `3P`, or `4P`
- **Modes:** `vs AI` (human vs AI opponents) or `Pass & Play` (local hotseat with a **Privacy Handoff Curtain** between turns)
- **Tools:** `💡 Hint` (`H`), `Xếp bài / Sort` (`S`), `Tutorial` (3-step walkthrough), `? Help`, and Mute (`🔊`/`🔇`)
- **Keyboard:** `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight` cycles card selection; `Escape` closes the Help modal.

## Persistence & Mute

Your best score, match statistics, mute preference, player count, and game mode are saved locally under `ta_la_phom_v1`.
