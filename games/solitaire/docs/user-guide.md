# User Guide — Solitaire (Klondike)

## Objective
Move all 52 cards into the **4 Foundation** piles (top right), building each suit up from **Ace to King**.

## Rules
- **Tableau (7 Columns):** Build downward in alternating colors (e.g., Red 6 on Black 7). Flip newly uncovered face-down cards automatically.
- **Empty Columns:** Only a **King** (or a stack beginning with a King) can move into an empty Tableau column.
- **Stock & Waste (Top Left):** Click the Stock pile to draw **1 card** (Turn 1) or **3 cards** (Turn 3). Click the empty Stock slot to recycle the Waste pile.

## Controls
- **Click:** Select a card or stack, then click a valid destination column or Foundation.
- **Double-Click (or click selected card again):** Quick-send a card to a matching Foundation.
- **Drag & Drop:** Drag any face-up card or stack to a valid pile.
- **Shortcuts:** `D` Draw Stock • `H` Hint • `Z` Undo • `P` Pause • `Esc` Clear selection / close modal.

## Learning & Assist Tools
- **? How to Play:** Opens the in-game rules and shortcut reference.
- **Tutorial:** Launches a 3-step guided practice deal covering Foundations, alternating stacks, and Kings.
- **Hint & Auto-Complete:** Highlights the best next move; once all Stock/Waste cards are gone and all Tableau cards are face-up, click **Auto-Complete** to finish automatically.

## Scoring
- Waste → Tableau: `+5`
- Any card → Foundation: `+10`
- Uncover face-down Tableau card: `+5`
- Foundation → Tableau: `-15`
- Recycle Stock (Turn 1): `-20` (score never drops below `0`)
