# User Guide — Cozy Town Tycoon

## Objective
Bake fresh pastries onto your display counter, earn coins as townsfolk buy them, unlock gourmet recipes, upgrade bakery equipment, and hire staff to automate production.

## Controls & Mechanics
- **Tap / Click** the bakery scene or **Tap to Bake** button (or press `Space` / `Enter` when focused) to bake your selected pastry.
- **Products:** Unlock **Butter Croissant** (75 coins) and **Berry Tart** (400 coins) for higher margins.
- **Upgrades (5 Tiers):** Boost tap bake power, display capacity, customer arrival speed, and tip chances.
- **Staff:** Hire **Apprentice Bakers** to auto-bake and **Friendly Cashiers** to boost sell prices.
- **Offline Earnings:** Automated bakeries earn coins while you're away (up to **8 hours**).

## Assist Tools & Settings
- **? Help:** Opens the in-game guide anytime.
- **Tutorial:** 3-step guided checklist to bake your first loaves and explore upgrades.
- **💡 Hint:** Recommends the best next purchase or action based on your current coins and stock.
- **Mute & Reset:** Toggle sound effects or reset your save from the top HUD.
