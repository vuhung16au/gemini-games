# Super Mario Bros. (1985) — NES

A browser recreation of Nintendo's 1985 side-scrolling platformer **Super Mario Bros.**, featuring **World 1 (`1-1`, `1-2`, `1-3`, `1-4`)** in Single-Player mode.

## Key Features
- **World 1 Complete (`1-1` to `1-4`):**
  - **1-1 Overworld:** Classic `?` blocks, Super Mushroom, hidden 1-UP, pipes with Piranha Plant, Goombas, Koopa Troopa, staircase, and Flagpole.
  - **1-2 Underground:** Cavern ceiling, vertical elevator platforms, coin rows, and Flagpole exit.
  - **1-3 Treetop Heights:** Elevated bridges, horizontal/vertical moving platforms, and Koopa patrols.
  - **1-4 Bowser's Castle:** Lava pits, rotating Fire Bars, Bowser breathing fireballs, and the collapsible Bridge & Axe leading to Toad's castle message.
- **Classic Physics & Power-Ups:** Momentum run/walk, variable jump height, Super Mushroom, Fire Flower (bouncing fireballs), Super Star, and 100-coin 1-UP.
