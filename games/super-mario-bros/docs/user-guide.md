# Super Mario Bros. (1985) — User Guide

## Objective
Guide Mario across **World 1-1 through 1-4**—collecting coins and power-ups, stomping enemies, and reaching each course's Flagpole (or Bowser's Castle Axe in `1-4`) before the timer hits zero.

## Controls (1-Player Mode)
- **Move:** `A` / `D` or `Left` / `Right` Arrow keys
- **Jump:** `W`, `Up` Arrow, `Space`, or `Z` (hold longer for higher jumps)
- **Sprint / Throw Fireball:** `Shift`, `X`, or `J`
- **Crouch:** `S` or `Down` Arrow (when Super or Fire Mario)
- **Pause:** `P` or `Escape`
- **Touch / Mobile:** On-screen Left/Right + `B` (Run/Fire) + `A` (Jump) buttons

## Power-Ups
- **Super Mushroom:** Grows Mario to Super Mario (breaks bricks and absorbs 1 hit)
- **Fire Flower:** Grants Fire Mario ability to throw bouncing fireballs
- **Super Star:** 10 seconds of invincibility
- **1-UP Mushroom / 100 Coins:** Grants +1 extra life

## Persistence
High score, highest reached World 1 stage, and Mute state persist automatically in `localStorage` (`super_mario_bros_v1`).
